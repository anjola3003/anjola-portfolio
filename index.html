01 / BUSINESS ANALYTICS

Sales & Profitability Analysis

Where are sales performing well, and where is profit being lost? Using a retail Superstore dataset, I explored sales, profitability, product categories, and regional performance to identify patterns that could inform better business decisions.

TOOLS USED

Excel   ·   SQL   ·   Tableau

$12.64M Total Sales
$1.47M Total Profit
11.62% Profit Margin

Key Findings

  • Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the product categories.
  • Furniture had the lowest category profit margin, at approximately 6.98%.
  • The Tables sub-category recorded an overall loss, highlighting an area for further investigation.
